# Smart Collections

Smart Collections are saved searches. They are populated dynamically whenever their contents are queried, as opposed to other types of collections whose contents are static. Smart Collections are created by clicking the save button on the search input.

## Using Smart Collections

Smart Collections can be assigned to slots in either Slot Editor or Time Slot Editor. Smart Collection contents are queried at scheduled generation and are used to fill the slot.

A filler list can also take its programs from a Smart Collection, so commercials, bumpers or trailers that match a saved search are added to filler without editing the list. See [Filler lists from a Smart Collection](/configure/library/filler#filler-lists-from-a-smart-collection).
